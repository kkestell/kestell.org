# Turkey Crab Sauce

## Ingredients

* 1/2 pint (1 cup) dairy sour cream
* 1 can crab meat
* 2 cups diced turkey or chicken
* 2 tablespoons sautéed onions (optional)

## Instructions

1. In a saucepan, combine the sour cream, crab meat, and diced turkey or chicken.
2. If using, sauté onions in a separate pan until golden and then add to the mixture.
3. Heat the mixture over medium heat until warmed through, stirring occasionally.
4. Serve hot over biscuits or in shells.
