# Anise Liqueur

## Ingredients

* 1 1/2 cups sugar
* 1 cup water
* 3 cups 80 proof vodka
* 4 teaspoons vanilla extract
* 4 teaspoons anise extract
* Yellow coloring (optional)

## Instructions

1. In a small saucepan, combine the sugar and water and cook over low heat until the sugar is completely dissolved.
2. Remove from heat and allow the syrup to cool completely.
3. Add the vodka, vanilla extract, and anise extract to the cooled syrup and stir to combine.
4. If desired, add a few drops of yellow food coloring.
5. Transfer the mixture to a bottle and seal tightly.
6. Store in a cool, dark place for at least 2 weeks before using.
7. Shake occasionally to ensure proper mixing.
