# Cherry Liqueur

## Ingredients

* 2 1/2 cups ripe sweet Bing cherries
* 4 cups sugar
* 4 cups water
* 2 cups 80 proof brandy
* 3 cups 100 proof vodka
* 1 teaspoon grated nutmeg (fresh)

## Instructions

1. In a large jar, combine the cherries, sugar, and water.
2. Stir to dissolve the sugar.
3. Add the brandy, vodka, and grated nutmeg to the jar.
4. Seal the jar tightly and store it in a dark, cool place for 1 week, shaking the jar every day.
5. After 1 week, strain the mixture and discard the cherries.
6. Transfer the liqueur to clean bottles and store in a cool, dark place.
