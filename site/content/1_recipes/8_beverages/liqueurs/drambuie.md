# Drambuie

## Ingredients

* 3/4 cup 80 proof scotch whiskey
* 1 1/2 tablespoons chopped angelica roots
* 1 cup orange clover honey (wild)

## Instructions

1. Place the scotch whiskey in a bottle or large jar.
2. Add the chopped angelica roots and stir to combine.
3. Allow the mixture to steep in a cool, dark place for 14 days, stirring occasionally.
4. After 14 days, add the honey and stir with a wooden spoon until well combined.
5. Store the liqueur in a dark, cool place for 1 month before using.
6. Shake occasionally to ensure proper mixing.
