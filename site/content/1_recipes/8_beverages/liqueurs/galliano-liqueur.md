# Galliano Liqueur

## Ingredients

* 1 cup sugar
* 1 cup water
* 3 1/2 cups 80 proof vodka (the cheapest you can buy)
* 3 1/2 tablespoons chopped angelica root
* 4 teaspoons nutmeg, grated
* 1 teaspoon cinnamon (fresh)
* 3 whole cloves

## Instructions

1. Make a syrup by boiling the sugar and water for 5-10 minutes.
2. Once the syrup is cool, combine it with the vodka, angelica root, nutmeg, cinnamon, and cloves in a bottle.
3. Shake well and store in a cool, dark place for at least 2 weeks before using.
4. Shake occasionally to ensure proper mixing.
