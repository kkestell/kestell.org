# Kahlua

## Ingredients

* 3 cups sugar
* 1 quart water
* 3 tablespoons instant coffee
* 3 1/2 teaspoons vanilla
* 3 cups 80 proof vodka

## Instructions

1. In a large pot, combine the sugar, water, and instant coffee.
2. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer for 5 minutes.
3. Remove from heat and allow the mixture to cool completely.
4. Stir in the vanilla extract.
5. Add the vodka and stir to combine.
6. Transfer the mixture to bottles and seal tightly.
7. Store in a cool, dark place for at least 2 weeks before using.
8. Shake occasionally to ensure proper mixing.
