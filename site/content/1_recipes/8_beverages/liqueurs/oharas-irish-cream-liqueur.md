# O'Hara's Irish Cream Liqueur

## Ingredients

* 1 cup Irish whiskey
* 4 oz can condensed milk
* 1 tablespoon cream of coconut
* 1 teaspoon Hershey's chocolate syrup
* 1 cup whipping cream

## Instructions

1. In a mixing bowl, combine the Irish whiskey, condensed milk, and cream of coconut.
2. Stir in the Hershey's chocolate syrup.
3. In a separate bowl, whip the cream until it forms soft peaks.
4. Gently fold the whipped cream into the whiskey mixture.
5. Refrigerate the mixture until well chilled.
6. Serve chilled and enjoy.
