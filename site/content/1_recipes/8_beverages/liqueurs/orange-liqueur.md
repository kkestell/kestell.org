# Orange Liqueur

## Ingredients

* 3 medium oranges (thinly pared, no white)
* 3 cups 80 proof vodka
* 1 1/4 cups sugar
* Orange color (optional)

## Instructions

1. Cut the orange peel into 8 pieces and place them in a large jar.
2. Add 2 cups of vodka to the jar and let it steep in a cool, dark place for 30-34 days.
3. After the steeping period, remove the orange peels and add the sugar.
4. Shake the jar until the sugar dissolves completely.
5. Add the remaining 1 cup of vodka and shake well.
6. Optionally, add orange color if desired.
7. Store the liqueur in a cool, dark place and shake occasionally.
