# Yellow Chartreuse Liqueur

## Ingredients

* 1 cup sugar
* 1 cup water
* 3 1/2 cups 80 proof vodka
* 3 teaspoons hyssop
* 3 teaspoons lemon balm
* 1 1/2 teaspoons angelica root, finely chopped
* 1 cinnamon stick, broken
* 1 teaspoon freshly grated nutmeg

## Instructions

1. Make a syrup by boiling the sugar and water for 5 minutes.
2. Allow the syrup to cool completely.
3. Add the vodka, hyssop, lemon balm, angelica root, cinnamon stick, and nutmeg to the cooled syrup.
4. Bottle the mixture and store it in a cool, dark place for at least 2 weeks before use.
5. Shake occasionally to ensure proper mixing.
