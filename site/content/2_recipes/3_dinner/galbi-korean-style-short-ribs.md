# Galbi (Korean Style Short Ribs)

## Ingredients

* 2 pounds Flanken ribs
* 5 tablespoons soy sauce
* 5 tablespoons Shaoxing wine
* 2 tablespoons honey
* 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
* 1 cup Asian (or Bosc) pear, peeled and cored
* 4 cloves of garlic
* 1/2 medium onion
* 1/2 teaspoon ginger
* 1 tablespoon toasted sesame oil

## Instructions

1. Combine marinade ingredients in a blender and blend until smooth.
2. Marinade short ribs between 8 and 12 hours.
3. Grill over extremely high heat until medium, about 2 minutes per side.
4. Top with thinly sliced green onions and toasted sesame seeds.
